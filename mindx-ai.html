MindX AI 7 Key Tasks

Participants are expected to follow the program commitments and maintain consistency throughout the learning journey.

01 · Class Attendance

Attend 100% of online classes and actively participate in the learning sessions.

02 · Daily Social Media Activity

Publish 3 social media posts every day as part of the program activity.

03 · Education System Outreach

Inform at least 30 people about the education program within 90 days.

04 · New Admissions Target

Secure 4 new admissions during the 30-day training period as specified in the program tasks.

05 · Daily Mentor Check-In

Make one phone call every day to your mentor for guidance and progress updates.

06 · In-Person Sessions

Attend a minimum of 3 physical sessions within 90 days.

07 · Commitment

Commit to the complete 3-month program and do not quit during the learning period.
